The problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a date whose day is 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.” It reaches every tenant, through the app, email and the booking-v2 form. This is the problem #4471 was named for, and the one the queued fix is for.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People do what the hint says and type the month first; the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only reports that came in through the form of a tenant on booking-v3 have this cause. The swap looks the same as parser, but the parser fix does not touch it: the form has to print the order it reads.

one-day: the date moves by one day

These reports are not swaps. The invite lands a day early or late, or people in different places get different days for the same meeting: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That points at time-zone conversion, not date order. The desk read them this way (desk-notes.json). Its cause table counts them where they are filed, under parser by their tenant, and so does the table above; they have their own ticket as well. The parser fix will not move them back.

Why one ticket held them all

  • Decision 0001 merges reports whose subjects match. Subjects describe the symptom (“Wrong day on the invite”), and every problem here has the same symptom. The same subject lines turn up under different problems.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The “intake tagged as” column shows it disagrees with the problem in every row; the tag is not a cause.
  • The export repeats some reports across overlapping pages. Counting rows instead of ids overstates the ticket.

What the desk does now

  • Keep #4471 for parser. Track hint and one-day in their own tickets, linked in the table.
  • Do not close the tickets when the parser fix ships; re-check this page against new reports first.
  • Merging on subject alone put different causes in one ticket. Changing that rule needs a new decision record that replaces 0001; this page does not change 0001.
